    About the survey data

    Source. Google Forms survey, one row per respondent. Names and phone numbers are removed at build time (scripts/build_primary_research.js); respondents appear only as R01, R02, …

    Multi-select questions ("select up to 3") are counted per option; percentages are of respondents, so bars can add up to more than 100%.

    Sample. Convenience sample — percentages are directional, not statistically representative. With a handful of respondents per segment, the cross-tab means are indicative only.

    Story outcomes ("found after effort", "not found", "unclear") are assigned by simple keyword rules on the free-text answer.

    How this dashboard was built

    Source. Google_Photos_Master_Reviews.csv — 117,037 deduplicated, verified reviews and community posts about Google Photos, harvested across 13 platforms (Play Store, App Store, YouTube, Hacker News, Reddit, GitHub, Stack Exchange, Google Support Community, Wikipedia).

    Sentiment. Rating-based: ★★★★–★★★★★ = Positive, ★★★ = Neutral, ★–★★ = Negative. Sources without a star rating (YouTube, Hacker News, forums) are marked Unknown rather than guessed.

    Themes. Rule-based keyword matching across 13 topics (backup, storage, crashes, UI, search, sharing, editing, video, deletion, notifications, login, missing photos, support). A review can match more than one theme; reviews matching none are grouped as "General feedback / other" (82.3% of the corpus) and excluded from the theme charts to keep them readable, though included in the theme × sentiment table.

    Severity score. volume × %negative — surfaces themes that are both common and disproportionately negative, not just the loudest ones.

    Keywords. Raw word frequency within negative reviews per theme, stripped of stopwords across English/French/German/Italian/Spanish/Indonesian (the corpus is multilingual) — expect some residual noise from short, ambiguous tokens.
